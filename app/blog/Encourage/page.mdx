# My Adventure With the Apple Student Swift Code Challenge 2026
### [Github Link](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1rh92yp/anthropic_has_opened_up_its_entire_educational/)
I was just hanging out with my friends on a random Saturday night in January, when I got an ad on Instagram for a Student Swift 
Code Challenge, by Apple. Having never made an App before, let alone touch Xcode or Swift, this opportunity stuck in my head for days
because if I were to ever give it a shot, I really couldn't think of a better time! So I marked it down on my calendar, and began ideating.

## Planning
---
Originally when I thought about ideas to make, I really wanted to make a game. Something like a 2-player Tower Defense game that requires two players
on one device, but when I really thought about the concept, it would take an incredible amount of assets and would be incredibly out of my field. Looking
at the requirements and Reddit chatter, the 25mb Zipped file submission sounded really tough, and at the time I didn't know how big that really could be.
I decided that I wanted to do something more simple, and with experience with [Taskify](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1rh92yp/anthropic_has_opened_up_its_entire_educational/)
