# My Adventure With the Apple Student Swift Code Challenge 2026
### [Github Link](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1rh92yp/anthropic_has_opened_up_its_entire_educational/)
I was just hanging out with my friends on a random Saturday night in January, when I got an ad on Instagram for a Student Swift 
Code Challenge, by Apple. Having never made an App before, let alone touch Xcode or Swift, this opportunity stuck in my head for days
because if I were to ever give it a shot, I really couldn't think of a better time! So I marked it down on my calendar, and began ideating.

## Planning
---
Originally when I thought about ideas to make, I really wanted to make a game. Something like a 2-player Tower Defense game that requires two players
on one device, but when I really thought about the concept, it would take an incredible amount of assets and would be incredibly out of my field. Looking
at the requirements and Reddit chatter, the 25mb Zipped file submission sounded really tough, and at the time I didn't know how big that really could be.
I decided that I wanted to do something more simple, and with experience with [Taskify](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1rh92yp/anthropic_has_opened_up_its_entire_educational/), I wanted 
to try to do something similar, on iOS. The birth of Encourage was mainly the thought of "hey, I could use an app that gameafies my day and forces me to lock in harder!"

## Thematics
---
Encourage has an extremely basic premise: connect your Apple Calendar, accept events you want to go to, and complete your day to earn trophies! To make this app better, I thought of creating themes
which would make the app more appealing than just an extension to Apple Calendar. Therefore, my girlfriend graciously help me create some gladiolus assets to add in to Encourage as trophies to mark
 days where a user would complete their day. I also created a colorblind-friendly color palette, and a collapseable Event Panel for a cleaner overall look.

## Inspiration
---
Throughout my college experience, I found myself at many times unable to encourage myself to get out of the house and organize all the tasks I have in during the day. I'd attempt to organize my events with third party apps like Calendar, Notion, etc. but although they helped, even with notifications and reminders like those, it felt like I began to tune them out, and as result consistently be late to events or miss them completely. Encourage began as a project to build upon those tools, intaking the Apple Calendar of the user and divesting ways to push the user to complete their tasks. By being able to visually see all your tasks in a day as a list, and confirming whether you really will go to those events or not builds a second layer of commitment. Encourage rewards the player in the form of visual completion, and a record of completion in the form of a flower trophy. These trophies are grander the more events you complete, and the overall theme of flowers symbolizing growth and nurturing your life to complete all your goals, with the flower of choice for Encourage being a Gladiolus, the flower of Strength.

## Accessibility
---
Accessibility was at the core of my UI design. With simple, distinct graphics that are easily comprehensible and spread out, it is something users with poor eyesight can still be able to view. With a collapsible events panel, this becomes more apparent as the completion ring also increases in size, allowing the viewer an easier time viewing the event. The colors chosen are also color-blind friendly, with the most information-dense section, the tutorial, having colors that follow the duke university colorblind palette(https://immunobiology.duke.edu/sites/default/files/2023-04/Colorblind-Palette.pdf, it ensures that information be able to be read by the maximum amount of users.

## Key Takeaways
---
I really enjoyed the process of creating Encourage. Claude was an extremely good source of learning for this project, as the setup, visualization and prototypes of ideas were all things
that Claude expediated. I think learning how to create a clean user experience, as well as trying to encorporate a theme(Game-afy, floral, colorblind accessible) were all challenges I enjoyed
giving myself, and completing. Getting input from friends, like to create a draggable event panel to create an even better experience was something I learned from my Web Dev class, and it paid off 
well. The greatest challenge was at the submission time, where I misunderstoof what a .swiftpm file meant. I thought a zip file would suffice of my project, but to have to create a .swiftpm file greatly made
it harder to submit, as there was issues with connecting to Apple Calendar, one of Encourage's key functionalities.
